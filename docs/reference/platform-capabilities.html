AZentis Notify · Platform Reference

Everything this platform can do

Two roles use this system, and each gets a very different set of powers. This is the full map — not a marketing summary — organized exactly the way the product itself is: what the platform owner can configure for any client, and what a client business can actually do once they're live.

Platform owner
Runs the ops console. Onboards, configures, and monitors every client business on one shared platform.
Client business
A tenant — fully isolated credentials, contacts, and history. Works in their own portal or their own backend via API.
Their customers
Receive real WhatsApp messages, reply, and control their own consent — never contacted without opting in.

As the platform owner

ops console

Everything below happens in the admin console — one place to bring on, configure, and watch over every client business, without touching code or redeploying anything.

Bringing a client on board
Create an isolated tenant
Name, slug, category, branding — a hard isolation boundary from every other client on the platform.
Connect their WhatsApp number
Paste credentials from a client's existing Meta setup, live-verified against Meta's API before anything saves.
Continue with Facebook Coming
One-click Embedded Signup once Meta approval completes — no manual credential copying at all.
Send a live test message
Confirm delivery and the reply loop both work before calling onboarding done.
Keeping every channel healthy
Submit ready-made templates
A starter library matched to the client's business category — pick, submit to Meta, done.
Sync approved templates
Pull in whatever's already live on a client's own WhatsApp Business Account.
Watch sending health
Daily usage against the tier ceiling and Meta's quality rating, per client, before either becomes a problem.
Rotate the master encryption key
Re-encrypt every client's stored credentials platform-wide without any downtime.
Governing access
Issue & revoke API keys
One key per client backend integration — shown once, then only its prefix, ever again.
Create portal accounts
Client team logins exist only because ops created them — no self-signup, ever.
Read the full audit trail
Every credential, branding, or key change, attributed to the admin who made it.
Suspend or archive a tenant
Pause a client's sending ability without deleting their history.
Extending a client's setup
Turn on AI auto-reply
First-line responses using the client's own OpenAI/Anthropic key, capped and handed off to a human deterministically.
Configure outbound webhooks
Push send/delivery/reply events into a client's own CRM or data warehouse — HMAC-signed, SSRF-guarded.
Enable MCP access
Let a client's own AI assistant (Claude Desktop, Cursor) read logs or send messages through the same guardrails.
Seeing the whole platform
Live analytics, any tenant
Sent/delivered/read/failed/replies, computed on every request — never a cached snapshot.
Full campaign detail
The exact message sent, per-recipient outcome, and every reply, for any campaign, any client.
A shared inbox, per tenant
See and reply to any client's customer conversations directly if they need the help.

As a client business

portal & API

Once connected, a client works in their own portal — or has their own backend call the same platform directly. Everything here is scoped to that one business; nothing crosses tenant lines.

Reaching customers proactively
Session messages
OTPs, task assignments, approvals, deploy alerts, generic alerts with buttons — within a 24h reply window.
Real broadcast campaigns
Send Meta-approved templates to a whole contact list, reachable even outside the 24h window.
Media & documents
Invoices, receipts, product photos, voice notes — just a public link, no pre-upload step.
Pick from starter templates
Pre-written, category-matched messages — submit for approval without writing copy from scratch.
Having real conversations
A shared inbox
Every customer reply as a conversation, assignable across the team, reply straight from here.
Every reply, correlated
Button taps and text replies both land against the exact message that triggered them.
Full campaign history
What was actually sent, who got it, who read it, and what they said back — one click away.
Growing your audience
Import & tag contacts
CSV import, tags like "VIP" or "trial-expiring", merge-on-import or replace-on-edit.
Build targeted lists
Filter by tag for the campaign you're actually sending, not your whole contact base.
Consent handled for you Automatic
STOP opts a customer out instantly and permanently blocks sends to them; START reverses it. You can't override it by accident.
Automating the busywork
AI first-line replies
Answer common questions automatically with your own OpenAI/Anthropic key, capped per conversation.
Deterministic human handoff
When the AI can't help or hits its cap, it hands off cleanly — never loops, never goes silent.
Push events to your own systems
Send/delivery/reply events land in your CRM or support desk the moment they happen.
Understanding your performance
Live delivery analytics
Delivery, read, and reply rates — recomputed on every load, never yesterday's numbers.
Sending health at a glance
Today's usage against your daily limit, your quality rating, and a nudge before you hit the ceiling.
A 14-day activity view
The dashboard you actually open every morning — sent, read, and reply trends over two weeks.
Connecting your own systems
A REST API, your key
/v1/send, send-bulk, opt-in, opt-out, logs — your backend, never our credentials.
Same guardrails, either path
Opt-in checks, quiet hours, and rate limits apply identically to the portal and to your API calls.
An AI assistant on your own key
Point Claude Desktop or Cursor at your API key to read logs, or send messages once you opt in — bulk send is never exposed this way.
What their customers actually experience
An authentic sender. Messages arrive under the business's real, reviewed display name.
A real conversation. Replies reach a person, not a void — same thread, either direction.
Consent that actually works. Reply STOP, get nothing further — no exceptions, no re-asking.
Never contacted cold. The platform blocks sends to anyone who hasn't opted in, even if they're on a list.
