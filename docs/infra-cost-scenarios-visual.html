Infrastructure cost: pilot, best case & worst case

What it costs to run AZentis Notify at three stages: validating with your first 4–5 clients, a production-grade lean deployment, and a scaled multi-tenant one.

September 2026 · figures are monthly, USD · see docs/production-db-analysis.md for the database-specific research behind these numbers

Stage 0 — Pilot (4–5 clients, validate first)

The goal here isn't maximum headroom, it's proving the product without overspending — most "best case" upgrades exist to solve problems this scale doesn't have yet.

Pilot total
$6–7 to $27 / mo
Range depends on one deliberate choice — see the Vercel row below
ComponentPilot costWhy this is fine at this scale
Database $0 Supabase Free, Mumbai Free tier only pauses after 7 days of zero activity. 4–5 live clients generate daily webhook traffic, so this never triggers — no performance compromise, genuinely production-fine here.
Redis $0 Not needed yet InlineQueueAdapter handles this volume synchronously with no noticeable delay. Redis solves a blocking problem you don't have at 5 clients.
File storage $0 Free tier Logos and light media for 5 tenants is nowhere near any free-tier ceiling.
Monitoring $0 Free tier, or skip Nice to have, not urgent at a scale you can personally watch.
apps/api hosting $6–7/mo Small VPS or Render Starter Worth paying even at pilot scale. A free tier that sleeps doesn't just run slower — it silently drops customer replies and STOP requests, damaging trust with the exact clients you're trying to prove the product to. Cheapest insurance in this list.
Vercel hosting $0 or $20/mo Hobby or Pro — your call Hobby's technical limits (bandwidth, execution) won't be touched by 5 clients — this isn't a capacity tradeoff. Hobby's terms restrict it to personal, non-commercial use, which this isn't, regardless of client count. A policy risk, not a performance one — Pro removes it for $20/mo.

The line item that can eclipse everything else Meta's WhatsApp conversation charges. A platform serving even a modest number of active businesses sending daily reminders can rack up more in conversation fees than in all hosting combined — and it's billed per tenant's WABA, not a hosting choice you control.
Watch this before it becomes a performance problem Database connection pooling. The moment concurrent tenants hit serverless functions at once, pooling becomes non-negotiable — without it, performance degrades exactly when you can least afford it.
The least negotiable line in the best-case column apps/api uptime. A "cheap" choice here (a free tier that sleeps) doesn't just slow a page — it silently drops delivery receipts and customer replies.
